\paragraph{Environments.} Scalar and coupled harnesses ran under per-directory
virtual environments (numpy/scipy). The stability analysis is analytic with a
numeric spot-check ($\eta=0.3$). Dates of the archived outputs are recorded in
the respective \texttt{results.md} / verification files; the paper cites those
archived outputs, and regeneration is expected to reproduce them to machine
precision (deterministic seeds: \texttt{seed=12345} in \texttt{baselines.py}).

\paragraph{Honesty notes carried from the harnesses.}
(i) The coupled verification reference was itself validated against the known
scalar exact solution before use. (ii) The regime-2 example uses an exact
integer ratio ($r=4$, $\rho=0$); fractional $\rho$ remains covered by the
leading-order bound only (Thm.~\ref{thm:remainder}). (iii) Page numbers in the
literature-pointer notes are approximate (form-feed counts); verbatim excerpts
are preserved in \texttt{notes/Di\_eq\_D\_literature\_pointers.md}.
